I extend van Benthem and Kerr's (2013) model of nature-based offset supply: landowners hold private information about the commercial value of their land and respond to a uniform baseline-price mechanism, but must also pay a privately known fixed cost to participate. The private information and the costly participation---acting alone---reduce the social value of such a market, but are not socially harmful. Together, hower, they can be.
